Every lake, a new puzzle

Deep in Lapland, reachable only by helicopter or a week's hike, lies one of Europe's last truly wild places. This six-day expedition was the goal of a two-year project, with a month each summer spent exploring the region and running first descents.

The original target was a remote side river feeding the lake system halfway along. Reaching it meant linking more than 100km of lakes, so the team rigged sails and let the wind carry them.

The real prize turned out to be the rapids between the lakes. Every outflow was a first descent: technical, committing and a puzzle no one had solved. Each one kept the team hungry for the next.

Broken gear, exhaustion and Arctic weather tested them throughout. The final push was 45km of flat water into a 40km/h headwind: 14 hours of waves and spray to reach the road.
