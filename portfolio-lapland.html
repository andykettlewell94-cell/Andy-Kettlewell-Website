Expedition, Lapland

Lapland, 67° north

Six days and more than 100km through one of Europe's last wild places, finishing with 14 hours into a headwind.

Where
Lapland, inside the Arctic Circle
Duration
6 days
Distance
More than 100km
Final push
45km, 14 hours into a headwind

One of Europe's last wild places

There are only a few truly wild places left in Europe, and this is one of them: deep in Lapland, in an area you can only reach by helicopter or a week of hiking.

The plan

Andy's friend David found the mission: paddle more than 100km, linking the area's lake systems to reach the river by the smoothest route.

Finding a six-day weather window inside the Arctic Circle is tricky, even in peak summer. With high winds forecast, teammate Ben had an idea: sail across the lakes and use the wind to their advantage, covering distance with almost no effort.

On the river

The weather changed every day, and so did the challenge. The team found their own food and ran rapids that had never been seen from a whitewater kayaker's point of view, using all their experience to stay safe on each descent between the lakes.

Along with plenty of challenging whitewater came the hardships: broken gear and exhaustion.

The final push

Then, on the last stretch, 45km of flat water out from the rapids, the weather turned. They could wait out the storm, or paddle the full 45km into a 40km/h headwind to get back to the camper vans.

They chose to paddle. For 14 hours, huge rolling waves crashed over the front of their boats while wind and spray hit them in the face, until they finally reached the nearest road and the vans.
