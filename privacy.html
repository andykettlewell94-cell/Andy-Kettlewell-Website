Privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information andykettlewell.co.uk collects, why, and what your rights are. In short: I only collect what you choose to send me, I use it to reply to you, and the site doesn't use cookies or tracking.

Who I am

This website belongs to Andy Kettlewell, based in Wales, UK. I'm responsible for your personal information under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018). For any privacy question, email andy.kettlewell94@gmail.com.

What I collect

When you use the contact form, I receive:

  • your first and last name
  • your email address
  • your phone number and company or organisation, if you add them
  • your message

If you email or phone me directly, I receive your contact details and whatever you include in your message.

How I use it

I use your details only to reply to your enquiry and, if we agree to work together, to arrange a talk, partnership or coaching. I won't add you to a mailing list, and I won't sell or share your details with anyone for marketing.

The legal basis is my legitimate interest in responding to people who contact me, or taking steps you've asked for before we enter into an agreement.

Services that handle your information

  • Formspree receives contact form messages and forwards them to my inbox. Formspree is based in the United States, so your message is transferred outside the UK. Formspree's privacy policy
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces on this site. To load them, your browser connects to Google, which receives your IP address. Google's privacy policy
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub may log your IP address when you visit, for security and to keep the service running. GitHub's privacy statement

Cookies and tracking

This site doesn't set cookies and doesn't use analytics or advertising trackers. Links to Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and LinkedIn take you to those sites, where their own privacy policies apply.

How long I keep it

I keep your messages for as long as I need them to deal with your enquiry and any work that follows, and no longer than two years after we were last in touch. Then I delete them.

Your rights

You can ask me to:

  • tell you what personal information I hold about you and give you a copy
  • correct anything that's wrong
  • delete your information
  • stop or limit how I use it

Email andy.kettlewell94@gmail.com and I'll respond within one month.

If you're unhappy with how I've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this policy

If I change how I handle personal information, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
