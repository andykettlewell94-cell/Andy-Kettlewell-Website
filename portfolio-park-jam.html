Bringing the stoke

Imagine turning up at your local snow park and finding Shaun White, Travis Rice and Mark McMorris there for the day, ready to jam, share the stoke, tell stories and give you tips on your tricks. That's what the Palm and Pyranha Park Jam tour does for kayaking.

Every year the brands send their athletes on a road trip around the UK's whitewater parks, and Andy has been part of the Palm team since 2022. Over around two weeks, the tour makes five to seven stops from London and Cardiff to North Wales, Northampton, Teesside and Glasgow, reaching as many paddlers and communities as possible. Through the summer, Park Jam events also head to Europe.

The team spends the days with young paddlers, coaches and clubs, sharing the joy of kayaking, helping people progress and throwing big tricks to give everyone something to aim for. Every year more than 200 people come along, all answering one simple question: why kayaking?

For Andy, the answer is the people. He comes home exhausted and buzzing, having watched the community grow stronger and the stoke run high. Kayaking gave him a club and a community when he was nine, and the Park Jam is his way of passing that on, making sure everyone who turns up feels part of it.
