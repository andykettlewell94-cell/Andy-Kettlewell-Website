The story

From the riverbank to the big water

Andy took his first strokes on the River Great Ouse in Bedford, aged nine. Slalom racing followed, taking him to the top 10 in the UK in C1, before he turned to coaching and became head coach for Canoe Wales.

He swapped the slalom gates for wild water: extreme races in Austria, expeditions across 28 countries, from the Arctic lakes of Lapland to the waterfalls of Iceland, and a 12-day self-supported descent of the Sary-Jaz in Kyrgyzstan. Since 2024 he has paddled full time, sharing the stoke at Park Jams, through his films and on stage.

“The river doesn't care about your plan. It rewards how you respond.”
