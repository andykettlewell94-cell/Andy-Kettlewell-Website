From the riverbank to the big water

I'm Andy Kettlewell. I've spent 23 years chasing whitewater, and every river has taught me something new about planning, commitment and staying calm when it counts.

This site is where I share the journeys, the lessons and the people who make it all possible.

“The river doesn't care about your plan. It rewards how you respond.”
